FAQ

Clear answers before you start.

Is this a distributor?

No. You would still have to release through a distributor of your choice. Track Record helps you prepare accurate metadata, agree splits and register your compositions, which a distributor does not do.

What is the difference between a distributor and a society?

A distributor delivers your recording to streaming services and pays recording (master) royalties. A collecting society collects royalties on the composition, such as radio, venues and streaming performances, for its members.

Do I need an ISRC or UPC before I start?

No. Distributors commonly issue these after you upload, and a society issues an ISWC once a work is registered. Leave them blank and add them later.

Do my co-writers need accounts?

No. They receive an emailed link to confirm their share. If they later sign up with the same verified email, the song appears in their Collaborations.

Can the app register my songs with a society for me?

Not yet. Societies use their own portals, so the app prepares what you need and explains the steps. Always confirm requirements with the society before paying or signing anything.

Is the registration information always up to date?

We date every country guide and link our sources, but this sector changes fast. Treat the guide as a starting point and confirm with the body itself.
