Royalty tracking

Know what your streams are likely worth, and who holds the rest.

Track Record turns your stream counts into an earnings estimate and shows which societies hold your royalties, in your own market and abroad, so you know where money is waiting before you go looking for it.

  • Estimates multiply your streams by an average rate per platform and show a low to high range
  • Platforms with no dependable rate say “No rate set” instead of guessing
  • See which society applies in your country, its status today and how to register
  • Rates are dated and sourced, and you can download the numbers as a PDF

How it works

From first draft to first royalty.

Every release you prepare teaches you something you will use on the next one.

  1. Step 1

    Prepare your release

    Enter your single, EP or album. Each field has a short explanation of what it is and why it matters.

  2. Step 2

    Agree the splits

    Add co-writers and their shares. We check the total is 100% and email each person a link to confirm, no account needed.

  3. Step 3

    Mark it ready

    Once the details check out, the release is locked. Hand it to your distributor, or let the extension copy it in for you.

  4. Step 4

    Register your rights

    Choose your country and see which society applies, what its status is today, and the steps to register your songs.

  5. Step 5

    Track streams and earnings

    Import stream counts, follow each platform month by month, and see what they are likely worth.

Features

What is inside

Everything between a finished song and a registered, trackable release.

Guided release forms

Singles, EPs and albums, with the fields distributors and collecting societies actually ask for.

Ownership splits

Composition and recording shares per song, validated to 100%, with legal names recorded for registration.

Co-writer confirmation

Co-writers get an emailed link to confirm, dispute or remove themselves. You see each answer in one place and can send reminders.

Performance tracking

Import stream counts, choose your platforms and see the trend, with PDF reports you can keep or share.

Royalty tracking

Earnings estimated from your streams, shown as a range, with the societies that hold your royalties.

Built-in glossary

Plain-language meanings of royalties, rights and identifiers, linked from the form fields where you meet them.

Registration guides

Which society to join, in which country, who is licensed right now, and what to bring. Dated and sourced.

Codes that arrive later

Distributors issue some codes after upload. Leave them blank now and add them when they arrive.

Distributor helper

A Chrome extension copies a ready release into your distributor's form. Get the extension.

A clearer picture

Know who does what.

Track Record sits alongside your distributor and your society. It does not replace them.

Delivering your music to streaming services

Track Record

Does not distribute. You keep your distributor of choice.

A distributor

Delivers your recording and pays recording (master) royalties.

Collecting composition royalties

Track Record

Shows which society applies and the steps to register.

A collecting society

Collects royalties on the composition, such as radio, venues and streaming performances.

Preparing accurate details

Track Record

Guided metadata, splits to 100%, and co-writer confirmation.

A distributor

Expects you to arrive with metadata and splits already settled.
