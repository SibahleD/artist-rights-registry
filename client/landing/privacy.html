Your privacy

Privacy statement

What we store, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Last updated 31 October 2026.

Who runs Track Record

Track Record is a tool that helps independent artists record who owns what on a release, so the details are ready for distributors and collecting societies. For any privacy question, write to s.dlamini2@alustudent.com.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, a scrambled (hashed) password, artist name, legal name, and the profile details you choose to add, such as country, nationality, contact information, links, a profile picture, and rights-society and publisher details.
  • Your releases: songs, credits, ownership splits, release dates and store links, plus the audio and cover files you upload.
  • Co-writer details you enter: a legal name, role, share and, if you want them to confirm, an email address.
  • Co-writer notices: the invitation we email on your behalf, when it was sent, and whether it was confirmed, disputed or removed. The notice link carries a secret code, and we keep only a scrambled version of it.
  • Glossary progress: which glossary terms you marked as understood or still learning.
  • Society registrations: the collecting societies you chose to track, with your status for each, an optional registration date and an optional member number. Only you can see this list.
  • Your preferences: email notices, and the analytics and play-count choices in Settings.
  • Sign-in: after you sign in, your browser keeps a sign-in token in its local storage for up to 7 days, or until you sign out. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers.

We use this information only to run the features you use: showing your releases, checking they are complete, sending co-writer invitations and reminders, and letting you export your data.

Who can see it

  • Co-writers you credit can see the song title, their own credit and the split you entered, through their emailed link or their own account. They can confirm, dispute, or remove themselves from the credit.
  • Nobody else can see your releases, files or profile.
  • Our service providers handle data for us only to run the service: hosting at Netlify and email sending at Supabase. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Co-writers who are not users

If you add someone's email to a split, we send them one message asking them to confirm their share. Their notice link expires after 14 days unless renewed. They do not need an account, and they can remove themselves or dispute the split from that page. If you are a co-writer and want your email address removed, options are provided .

Analytics

Analytics and play counts are off by default and are not collected yet. If they are added, they will only include you if you switch them on in Settings, and you can switch them off again at any time.

This website and the extension

  • This website sets no cookies. It reads your browser's local storage only to see whether you are already signed in, so it can show "Open the app" instead of "Log in". It loads the Poppins font from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when a page loads.
  • The Track Record helper extension runs only when you press a button, on the page you are viewing. It has no access to the rest of your browsing and sends no telemetry. It keeps your sign-in token in session storage, which is dropped when the browser closes, and stores your distributor on/off choices in the browser.

Your choices

  • See and download your data: Settings, Security and privacy, Download my data. The file has your profile, links, releases with credits, and notices. It leaves out audio, images and co-writer email addresses.
  • Correct it: change your profile, sign-in details, releases and splits at any time while a release is still a draft.
  • Delete your account: Settings, Delete account. This removes your profile, links, draft releases, uploaded files and sign-in tokens, and signs you out everywhere. Releases marked ready or released must be moved back to draft and deleted first.

When you delete your account, a blank placeholder record remains (no name, no email, no password) because other people's records, such as a split dispute, may point to it. Where you were credited on someone else's song, the name they typed stays, but it is no longer linked to you, and any email address saved for you on that credit is cleared.

Security

  • Passwords are stored only in hashed form, so we cannot read them.
  • Uploaded files and releases are visible only to your signed-in account and, for the parts that concern them, to the co-writers you credit.
  • Connections are encrypted with HTTPS.

Age and the law

The service is meant for adults who release music. We aim to follow Rwanda's data protection law (Law No. 058/2021) and will update this statement if the law or the service changes.
