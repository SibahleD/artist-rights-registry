Track Record helper: stop retyping your release.

When a release is marked ready, the helper copies its details into your distributor's upload form. You review everything and press submit yourself.

Download the extension How to install

ZIP · version 0.3.0 · for Chrome 108 or newer

What it fills in

Title, artists, dates, genre, songs and credits.

The helper copies the title, artists, dates, genre, ℗ and © lines, songs, credits and ISRCs from a ready release into the matching boxes, and lists anything it could not find so you can fill it by hand.

Installation

Install it in four steps

It is not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it yourself. It takes about a minute.

  1. Step 1

    Download and unzip

    Download the zip above and unzip it. You get a folder called track-record-helper.

  2. Step 2

    Open Chrome's extensions page

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and switch on Developer mode (top right).

  3. Step 3

    Load it unpacked

    Choose Load unpacked and pick the track-record-helper folder. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu so it is one click away.

  4. Step 4

    Connect your account

    Open the extension, enter the API address https://your-site/api and sign in with your Track Record account. Pick a ready release, open your distributor's upload page and press Fill release details.

What it does

  • Fills text boxes and drop-downs from your ready release
  • Picks yes or no answers, and presses “Add artist” style buttons to make room for names
  • Shows a preview of every value, each with a Copy button
  • Lists anything it could not find, so you fill it by hand

What it never does

  • Submit the form or tick terms and conditions
  • Upload your audio or artwork
  • Touch login, payment or bank fields
  • Run in the background or track your browsing

The helper only runs when you press a button, and only on a distributor you have switched on in its popup. Real distributors start switched off, so read your distributor's terms on automation first. This is an early version: it has been tested on a practice page that copies the RouteNote layout, so expect to check each field.
